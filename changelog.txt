Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-08-05
  Features:
    - Split early fluid logistics into lead and copper pipe, underground-pipe, and pump families that can run beside one another without connecting.
  Balance:
    - Audited more than 600 FluxWorks-owned or modified recipes and reduced ordinary recipes to six ingredient types or fewer.
    - Reworked orbital, planetary, reactor, combat, logistics, electrical, AAI Industry, and native FluxWorks recipes around representative assemblies instead of repeating their subcomponents.
    - Kept seven ingredients only for Promethium Science and Converged Quantum Processor recipes, where the width represents four independent planetary production lanes.
  Changes:
    - Removed the single-use Ceramic Wafer item and recipe, folded its ceramic role into Memory Die, and added a migration to Ceramic Insulators.
    - Restored Flux Harvester conversion recipes to the familiar destination-item graphics instead of crushed or processed variants.
    - Prevented broad compatibility from appending redundant generic motors to explicitly curated machine recipes.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed Easy and Hard Ion Storm presets failing a validator that assumed Normal-mode lightning energy.
    - Fixed Skip burner stage failing validation after it correctly bypassed Fluid Mining's hidden Basic Fluid Handling prerequisite.
  Diagnostics:
    - Tightened FluxWorks recipe complexity validation to six ingredients, with an explicit convergence exception.
    - Added complexity coverage for curated shared recipes and validation for both independent pipe networks.

Version: 0.1.3
Date: 2026-08-04
  Features:
    - Added the Skip burner stage startup setting, disabled by default, to hide burner technologies and begin with electrical infrastructure when enabled.
  Progression:
    - Removed seven reported bootstrap softlocks involving refined silicon, wet stone mining, power regulators, alumina refractory, oil refining, plastic, and advanced circuits.
    - Moved basic fluid handling, steam power, military, defenses, radar, lamps, and rail transport into practical early-game positions.
    - Reduced Electricity to 10 automation science packs and made the early steam path use short automation-science milestones.
    - Removed cryogenic science from Propellant Synthesis and Flux Catalysis.
    - Made crushed-ore smelting available with Ore Crushing and retired the redundant Dense Ore Smelting technology.
    - Retired the empty duplicate Precision Alloys technology and removed duplicate early unlocks.
    - Locked Promethium Impact Field mining behind Purple Flux extraction.
  Balance:
    - Simplified early boiler, railway, military, radar, wall, lamp, pole, refinery, solar-panel, and fluid-infrastructure recipes.
    - Increased rail recipe output and removed inappropriate advanced components from basic infrastructure.
    - Rounded FluxWorks research costs and times to clean increments of five.
  Changes:
    - Retired unused copper tubes and cleaned their remaining recipe references.
    - Separated bootstrap electricity unlocks from advanced fabrication components.
    - Ordered crushed ores consistently in the Intermediate Products crafting tab.
    - Prevented Industrial Methods and Systems Analysis science packs from being handcrafted.
  Graphics:
    - Replaced the mod thumbnail with the new four-color Flux gear over the Shattered Planet.
    - Replaced the Material Handling technology icons with distinct FluxWorks artwork.
    - Corrected the Acid Synthesis recipe icon to show sulfuric acid.
  Locale:
    - Added the missing Material Handling technology names and updated affected descriptions.
  Bugfixes:
    - Removed duplicate technology prerequisites when retired progression nodes converge on the same replacement.
    - Corrected coal crafting-tab placement, duplicate iron-beam unlocks, rubber-sheet valuation, and excessive Fluid Handling unlocks.
  Diagnostics:
    - Added regression checks for the reported softlocks, burner-stage setting, recipe ownership, unlock placement, and endgame Promethium mining gate.
